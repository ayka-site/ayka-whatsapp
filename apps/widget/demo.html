📋 Embed on Your Website

Add this single script tag before </body> to embed AyKa on any website:

<script src="https://your-api.ayka.in/widget/embed/ayka-widget.js"
  data-business-id="YOUR_BUSINESS_ID"
  data-api-url="https://your-api.ayka.in"
></script>

Replace YOUR_BUSINESS_ID with the client's ID from the dashboard. That's it — the widget auto-loads branding, theme, and AI config.

👇 Click the chat bubble in the bottom-right corner to try it!
